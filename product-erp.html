Project, billing, and site management in one system — built around how Indian contractors actually run projects: running bills, measurement sheets, material reconciliation, and site progress in one place instead of scattered spreadsheets.

What it's being built to do

Running bills & measurement sheets

Item-rate and measurement-based billing that follows government and client contract formats, with revision history.

Multi-site project tracking

One dashboard across sites — progress, manpower, and material status without switching spreadsheets per project.

Material & purchase reconciliation

Purchase orders, delivery challans, and site consumption tied together so stock and cost stay in sync.

NCRs, claims & documentation

Standard formats for non-conformance reports, rate disputes, and claim documentation, drafted from site data.

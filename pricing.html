Questions

What happens after the 14-day trial?+

You pick a plan and we send a payment link — your data and settings carry over exactly as they were, nothing is reset. If you don't continue, your data stays safe for 30 days in case you change your mind, then it's deleted.

Can I switch plans later?+

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime; the change applies from your next billing cycle, and we'll prorate the difference.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?+

No setup fee on Starter or Growth. Enterprise plans that need custom data migration or integrations are scoped and quoted upfront, with nothing hidden.

How is a "guard" counted?+

Each unique guard profile in Guard Details counts once, regardless of how many sites or shifts they cover. Site Incharge, Security Agency and admin/office logins never count toward your guard total.

Where is our data stored?+

On Firebase, in an Asia-Pacific data region. Your organisation's data is isolated from every other organisation on Secure — nothing is ever shared or mixed between customers.
